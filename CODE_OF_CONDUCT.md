# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly
representing the project, including its repository, issue tracker, pull
requests, and community discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
code. Report concerns privately through the contact information on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bwente). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
