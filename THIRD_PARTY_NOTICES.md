## amCharts World Outline map

`worldOutlineLow.svg` contains geographic map data adapted from the amCharts
World Outline SVG map:

- Creator and attribution party: [amCharts](https://www.amcharts.com/)
- Source: [World Outline SVG map](https://www.amcharts.com/svg-maps/?map=worldOutline)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

The source map was modified for MMM-WorldClockMap by converting it into a
standalone SVG path, presenting it in a cropped Mercator view, and changing its
visual styling for a dark MagicMirror display. The module adds a calculated
day/night overlay, grid, city markers, and labels at runtime; those additions
are not part of the amCharts source map.
